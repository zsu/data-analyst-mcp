# Routing: which sources answer which questions

Generated by tools/gen_routing.py from each pack's policy.json; do not edit by hand.

Split the question into measures, give each its source, then read only those packs' source.md.
Use `fetch.py --route "<question>"` for a ranked suggestion.

## Usual pairings

| Question | Primary | Cross-check / context | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| An address or a point to census geographies (FIPS, tract, district) | census-geocoder | census, cdc, hud, fema | Geocode first, then join by FIPS code; match the geography vintage to the data's year. |
| Federal debt, deficit, interest costs | treasury | bea, fred, cbo publications via govinfo | Debt and deficits from Treasury (MTS, Debt to the Penny); GDP from BEA (fiscal-year GDP from quarters) for shares. |
| Market interest rates, yield curve | treasury-yields | fred | Treasury par yields; FRED for long histories and policy rates. Average rates on outstanding debt are in treasury. |
| GDP, income, consumption, PCE inflation | bea | fred | BEA is the producer; FRED for vintages (ALFRED). |
| Jobs, unemployment, payrolls, JOLTS | bls | dol, fred | BLS CPS/CES/JOLTS; DOL weekly UI claims; FRED for long series. |
| Consumer prices, inflation, real dollars | bls | bea, fred | CPI-U from BLS; PCE price index from BEA; name the deflator. |
| Wages and earnings | bls | ipums, opm | BLS ECI, CES, OEWS; IPUMS CPS/ACS for distributions by group (weighted, with SEs). |
| Federal workforce: headcount, hires, separations, pay | opm | bls, ipums, usaspending | OPM FWD (coverage gaps: USPS, intelligence agencies; pay withheld for ~45% of records); BLS ECI or IPUMS for private comparisons. |
| Demographics, poverty, income by place | census | ipums, bea | ACS tables with margins of error; ACS PUMS (census) or IPUMS microdata for custom tabulations; BEA regional income. |
| Custom tabulations from recent ACS or CPS microdata | census | ipums, bls | ACS PUMS (2004 on, PWGTP1-80 replicate SEs), CPS basic monthly (1989 on) and ASEC (1992 on; 160 replicate SEs from 2005) from Census, public domain; IPUMS for harmonized codes across years, CPI99, CPS panels, earlier years. |
| Custom tabulations from survey microdata: long series, NHIS, MEPS, ATUS, historical censuses | ipums | census, bls | IPUMS CPS/ACS/decennial, NHIS, MEPS; weighted estimates with SEs; microdata never redistributed. SIPP through census. |
| Income and program participation month to month (SNAP, SSI, Medicaid, coverage spells) | census | ipums, medicaid | SIPP microdata (person-months; weight by WPFINWGT; reference year = file year - 1). |
| Federal budget by account or function, historical tables, credit subsidy rates | omb | treasury, usaspending | OMB Historical Tables and Public Budget Database by budget edition (each revises history); Treasury MTS for actual monthly totals. |
| Social Security and Medicare trust funds, national health spending projections | actuaries | treasury, cms, medicaid | Trustees Reports (intermediate assumptions) and CMS NHE; SSA tables by manual download. |
| Medicaid and CHIP enrollment, spending, drug use | medicaid | cms, census | data.medicaid.gov; preliminary vs updated rows; recent CMS-64 expenditure reports are manual downloads. |
| Income tax returns, AGI and migration by state, county, ZIP code | irs-soi | census, bea | SOI amounts in thousands; tax year vs filing year; small cells suppressed. |
| State and local government taxes, finances, pensions | census | pbgc, bea | Census QTAX (quarterly) and annual government finances; fiscal years differ by government. |
| Imports, exports, tariffs and customs duty | census | usitc, treasury, world-bank | Census international trade by HS code with calculated duty; USITC HTS for statutory rates by line; Treasury MTS customs duties for receipts. |
| Economic forecasts and forecast benchmarks | philfed | fred, usda-outlook | SPF and Greenbook (5-year lag) forecasts; USDA WASDE and baseline for agriculture. |
| Household balance sheets, consumer credit, bank lending surveys | fed-board | fred, nyfed | Fed Board Z.1, G.19, SLOOS, SCF; FRED for single-series histories. |
| Farm income, crop supply and demand outlook | usda-outlook | usda-nass | WASDE projections, ERS farm income forecasts, USDA baseline; NASS for survey estimates. |
| Immigration: green cards, naturalizations, refugees, enforcement | dhs-ohss | census | OHSS Yearbook by fiscal year; counts are events, not people. |
| Veterans: population, benefits, VA spending by place | va | census, usaspending | VetPop is a projection model; GDX for expenditures by state and district. |
| Student loans: portfolio, delinquency, repayment plans, volume by school | fsa | college-scorecard, treasury | FSA data center (.xls); recipients are not additive across programs. |
| Private pension insurance | pbgc | fed-board | PBGC data tables; single- and multiemployer programs are separate. |
| House prices and conforming loan limits | fhfa | fred, hud | FHFA HPI flavors differ; loan limits by county and units. |
| Unemployment insurance trust funds | dol | treasury | ETA 2112 state trust fund flows and balances; Treasury for Title XII advances and federal accounts. |
| TANF and child care program spending and caseloads | acf | usaspending, census | ACF files are manual downloads (the site refuses scripts). |
| Health insurance, access to care, health care spending by group | ipums | census, cdc, cms | IPUMS NHIS (insurance, access, conditions) and MEPS (spending by payer); ACS tables for state coverage; CMS for Medicare program data. |
| Federal spending by agency, program, place, recipient | usaspending | treasury, census | Obligations vs outlays differ; Treasury MTS for totals; Census population (not USAspending's) for per-person figures. |
| Legislation, votes, laws, CRS reports | congress | govinfo, federal-register | Congress.gov for bills and actions; GovInfo for text and CBO/committee reports; Federal Register for rules. |
| Regulations and rulemaking | federal-register | regulations, govinfo | Rules and notices; Regulations.gov for dockets and comments. |

| Drugs, devices, recalls | fda | clinical-trials, nih | Adverse-event reports are not incidence rates. |
| Research funding and trials | nih | clinical-trials | NIH RePORTER projects; ClinicalTrials.gov registrations. |

| Company financials and filings | sec | fred | EDGAR XBRL facts; SEC asks for a contact User-Agent. |
| Housing costs and assistance | hud | census, fred | HUD FMRs and income limits; ACS rents and values. |
| Agriculture | usda-nass | bls, world-bank | NASS Quick Stats. |
| SNAP, WIC and school meals: participation, costs, state policy options | usda-fna | census, usaspending | FNA Program Data (monthly, by state); SNAP State Options Report; State Activity Reports (FY 2019-2023 tidy). |
| Characteristics of SNAP households: income, earnings, children, elderly, benefit amounts | snapqc | usda-fna, ipums | SNAP QC public-use microdata (FY 2014 on), weighted with FYWGT; caseload totals from usda-fna; IPUMS CPS/ACS for SNAP-reporting households. |
| Transportation | bts | nhtsa | BTS statistics; NHTSA for vehicle safety. |
| Environment, air and water quality | epa | epa-aqs, usgs, noaa | EPA envirofacts/ECHO; AQS monitor data; USGS water. |
| Weather and climate | noaa | nws | NOAA NCEI history; NWS is forecasts and the last week of observations only. |
| Earthquakes, water, hazards | usgs | fema | USGS events and water; FEMA declarations and claims. |
| Disasters and insurance | fema | usaspending, census | Declarations, NFIP, public assistance. |
| Crime and justice | fbi | doj-news, census | NIBRS transition: participation changed sharply in 2021. |
| Higher education | college-scorecard | naep, census | Scorecard institution data. |
| K-12 achievement | naep | census | NAEP estimates have standard errors; test significance. |
| Federal contracting labor rates | gsa-calc | usaspending | CALC+ ceiling rates. |
| Food composition and nutrients | usda-fooddata | cdc | FoodData Central; one row per food and nutrient. |
| Patents and trademarks | uspto | sec | USPTO Open Data Portal (key needs a USPTO.gov account). |
| Renewable energy resources | nrel | eia | NREL resource and incentive data. |
| International comparisons | world-bank | bea, fred | World Bank indicators: check currency and base year. |
| Long histories, many indicators at once, data vintages | fred | the producing agency's pack | FRED re-publishes; cite the original source too; ALFRED for as-of vintages. |

## Use instead

Questions a pack does not answer, and the pack that does (each pack's policy.json `use_instead`).

| Not in | Question | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| bea | CPI, jobs, unemployment, wages by occupation | bls | BLS produces these. |
| bea | Budget data by fiscal year | treasury | Treasury Fiscal Data. |
| bea | Market interest rates | treasury-yields | Daily Treasury yields. |
| bls | PCE prices, GDP, personal income | bea | BEA produces the national accounts. |
| bls | Worker microdata for custom groups | ipums | IPUMS CPS with weights and SEs. |
| bls | Federal civilian employees | opm | OPM's Federal Workforce Data. |
| census | Monthly unemployment and payroll jobs | bls | BLS produces both. |
| census | GDP and personal income by state | bea | BEA regional accounts. |
| census | Microdata series harmonized across decades, ASEC standard errors before 2005, NHIS, MEPS or historical censuses | ipums | IPUMS codes each variable the same way across years and carries ASEC replicate weights for every year; ACS PUMS and single CPS files (ASEC replicates from 2005) come from this pack. |
| census | International comparisons | world-bank | World Bank indicators across countries. |
| census | Federal spending by state | usaspending | USAspending awards by place of performance. |
| cfpb | Bank financial condition or failures | fdic | FDIC BankFind holds call-report financials and the failed-bank list. |
| cfpb | Company financial statements | sec | EDGAR XBRL company facts. |
| cfpb | Auto safety complaints | nhtsa | NHTSA vehicle safety complaints, not consumer-finance complaints. |
| fed-board | One Board series' long history or its past vintages | fred | FRED mirrors most Board series; ALFRED keeps vintages. |
| fed-board | SOFR, EFFR, SOMA holdings, primary dealer positions | nyfed | The New York Fed publishes these. |
| fed-board | Forecasts of these series | philfed | SPF and Greenbook forecasts. |
| fed-board | Bank-level balance sheets and income | fdic | FDIC institution financials. |
| fhfa | One FHFA house price series for the U.S. or a state, with no index flavor choice | fred | FRED carries the all-transactions and purchase-only HPI series under stable IDs. |
| fred | BLS detail with preliminary flags, footnotes and gaps | bls | The bls pack keeps BLS's own flags. |
| fred | BEA tables with line numbers and vintages | bea | The bea pack reads BEA tables as published. |
| fred | Treasury daily statements and auctions | treasury | Treasury Fiscal Data. |
| ipums | A tabulation from one or a few recent ACS PUMS years, one CPS month or one CPS ASEC year (standard errors from 2005 on) | census | The census pack serves the same Census files with the census key, in seconds, public domain (shareable), with ACS PUMS replicate weights and the ASEC's from Census's replicate weight file. |
| treasury | Market yields and the daily par yield curve | treasury-yields | Daily Treasury par yield curve rates. |
| treasury | Historical budget tables in percent of GDP, the President's budget | omb | OMB Historical Tables by budget edition. |
| treasury | State and local government finances | census | Census government finance surveys. |
| usitc | Duties actually collected, import values and quantities by HS code and country | census | Census international trade reports import values and calculated duty; the HTS gives the statutory rates only. |
| usitc | Total customs duties received by the Treasury, monthly or by fiscal year | treasury | The Monthly Treasury Statement reports customs duty receipts. |
